# MolmoBot Feasibility Artifacts

Representative rollout clips captured during the sim-only feasibility work.

Files:
- `smoke_success_exo.mp4`: exo-style view from the 1-episode smoke benchmark that completed successfully.
- `pick_subset_success_exo.mp4`: exo camera view from a successful episode in the 10-episode pick-only subset.
- `pick_subset_failure_exo.mp4`: exo camera view from a failed episode in the 10-episode pick-only subset.
- `rby1_door_smoke_20260407/`: first successful 1-episode RBY1 door smoke run.
- `rby1_doorplusopen_small_20260407/`: reusable 3-episode RBY1 door validation with both success and failure examples.
- `rby1_pnp_blocker_20260407/`: reusable RBY1 pnp validation blocker log and manifest.

These are small illustrative artifacts only. The full benchmark outputs remain under `/tmp` and are documented in [00_interim_results.md](./../../00_interim_results.md) and [02_gpu_benchmark_log.md](./../../02_gpu_benchmark_log.md).
