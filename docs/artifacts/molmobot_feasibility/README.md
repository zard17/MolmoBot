# MolmoBot Feasibility Artifacts

Representative rollout clips captured during the sim-only feasibility work.

Files:
- `smoke_success_exo.mp4`: exo-style view from the 1-episode smoke benchmark that completed successfully.
- `pick_subset_success_exo.mp4`: exo camera view from a successful episode in the 10-episode pick-only subset.
- `pick_subset_failure_exo.mp4`: exo camera view from a failed episode in the 10-episode pick-only subset.

These are small illustrative artifacts only. The full benchmark outputs remain under `/tmp` and are documented in [`franka_droid_feasibility_status_20260406.md`](/MolmoBot/docs/franka_droid_feasibility_status_20260406.md).
